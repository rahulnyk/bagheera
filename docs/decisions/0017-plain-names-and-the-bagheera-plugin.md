# Plain names, a few Jungle Book names, and commands under the `bagheera` plugin

## Context and problem statement

What are commands, stages and parts called?

## Decision outcome

Chosen by the owner.

* Commands, skills, agents, files and workflows get plain names. A few top-level parts may get a themed name, proposed to the owner first and listed in the [naming register](../design.md#naming-register).
* The plugin is `bagheera`, and its commands are `/bagheera:<command>`, always written in full. `/bagheera` on its own says where a feature stands and gives the next command.
* The stages are Spec, Plan, Build and Wiki, in the pages and the portal. Their commands are `/bagheera:spec`, `/bagheera:plan`, `/bagheera:build` and `/bagheera:update-wiki`.
* `seeonee` is the placeholder GitHub org.

### Consequences

* Good, because a new joiner can guess what each thing does.
