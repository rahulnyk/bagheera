# The pilot proves the flow works end to end, on at least 10 tickets

## Context and problem statement

What does the pilot have to show before more teams adopt Bagheera?

## Decision outcome

Chosen by the owner. The pilot proves the concept works; efficiency is measured once it does.

* One team, at least 10 real tickets.
* **Targets:** each spec is drafted in the portal and approved by its PM before Build, then built and merged; each PR updates the wiki.
* **Noted, not targets:** rounds of questions, conflicts found while drafting, specs changed after approval, pre-flight stops, and costs.

### Consequences

* Good, because the pilot answers one question: does it work?
* Bad, because claims about efficiency wait until after the pilot.
